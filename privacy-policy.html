Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026 · NebulaScan v1.0

1. What We Collect

NebulaScan collects only the URL you submit for analysis. We record the URL, the resulting trust score, risk verdict, and the timestamp of the scan. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identifier
  • Your IP address (not stored in the database)
  • Browser fingerprints or device information
  • Cookies or persistent tracking tokens

2. How We Use Your Data

Submitted URLs are used solely to:

  • Perform fraud and phishing analysis (SSL check, WHOIS lookup, blacklist check)
  • Return a trust score and risk verdict to you
  • Cache the result for 24 hours to improve performance for repeated queries
  • Display aggregate statistics (total scans, risk counts) on the dashboard

We do not sell, share, or monetise your data in any form.

3. Zero Trust Data Handling

NebulaScan is built on Zero Trust principles for data handling:

  • Anonymous by design — scan records carry no user identity. History cannot be traced back to any individual.
  • sessionStorage only — scan results are passed between pages via sessionStorage, which is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab. Nothing is written to localStorage or cookies.
  • Minimal retention — we retain only what is necessary to display history and statistics. No raw request logs are kept.
  • Deletable history — scan records can be removed at any time via the API.

4. Third-Party Services

When you submit a URL, NebulaScan may forward it to the following external services for analysis:

  • Google Safe Browsing API — to check for known malware and phishing threats
  • WHOIS lookup — to determine domain registration age
  • SSL certificate check — performed directly against the target domain
  • CoinGecko API — for Bitcoin market data (no URL data is sent)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. NebulaScan does not control what those services do with the data they receive.

5. Data Storage

Scan records are stored in a local SQLite database (nebula.db) on the server. This database is not exposed to the internet and is accessible only by the server process running NebulaScan.

6. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is no personal profile to access, correct, or delete. If you wish to remove a specific scan record, it can be deleted via the scan history API. All scan data can be cleared entirely on request.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of NebulaScan after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
